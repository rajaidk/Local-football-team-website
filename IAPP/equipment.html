Mandatory Equipment Checklist

Please make sure you have all the required gear before stepping onto the pitch. Safety and preparation come first!

Football Boots

Firm ground (FG) cleats or turf shoes are required. No metal studs are allowed on our artificial pitches to prevent damage and injury.

Shin Guards

Strictly mandatory for all players during training and matches. You will not be allowed to play without proper shin protection.

Training Kit

All members must wear lightweight, quick-dry athletic wear. Official APU United home or away colors are preferred for weekend sessions.

Water Bottle

Hydration is key. Bring your own labeled water bottle to every session. Sharing is heavily discouraged for health and hygiene reasons.
