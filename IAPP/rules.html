Club Rules & Fair Play

To make sure everything runs smoothly on the field, all members of APU United Football Club must follow these rules. Football is a team sport, and we expect everyone to show respect to their teammates, opponents, and referees.

1. General Conduct

  • Punctuality: Please arrive at least 15 minutes before the match or training session starts.
  • Respect: Treat all players and coaching staff with respect. Bullying or discrimination of any kind is strictly not allowed.
  • Facilities: Keep the stadium and pitch clean. Throw all your rubbish into the provided bins after the game.

2. On-Pitch Fair Play

  • Referee Decisions: The referee's decision is final. Do not argue with the referee during the match.
  • No Dangerous Play: Slide tackles from behind are completely banned in friendly and practice matches to prevent injuries.
  • Language: No bad words or aggressive shouting on the pitch. Keep the communication positive.

3. Health and Safety

  • Warm-ups: All players must join the team warm-up before a match or training session to prevent injuries.
  • Reporting Injuries: If you get hurt during a game, you must tell the coach immediately. Do not force yourself to keep playing.
  • Hydration: Please bring your own water bottle to the pitch. For hygiene reasons, sharing water bottles is not allowed.
